About ErosCorp

ErosCorp is a Greek perfumery company inspired by mythology and Mediterranean tradition. Its name references Eros, the god of love, symbol of passion, beauty and desire — elements that directly shape the brand identity. ErosCorp stands out for using natural raw materials and valuing artisan traditions, combined with modern production technology. Its products are designed for both men and women, with fragrance lines ranging from fresh to intense and sensual.
